playing with Gender Pay Gap Data

22:51 - 15/04/2026

this is a nice short one - this is moreso just saying "hey, i made a thing!". if you want to see the thing, you can just go here

If you were not aware, in the UK it's a legal duty for employers with 250+ employees to report data on the gender pay gap. As per a handy page on the UK gov's site, this data is made up of:

  • mean (average) and median gender pay gap for hourly pay and bonus pay

  • percentage of men and women in each hourly pay quarter

  • percentage of men and women receiving bonus pay

What's really fun here is this data is provided to us under the OGL (Open Government License), and, every year's data can be downloaded as a CSV file that's only four megabytes in size. As such... we can play around with this data in interesting ways!

For this tool - a friend posted in a couple of chats I'm in about companies in/around games, specifically comparing their pay gap data to last year. this then left me wanting to compare over multiple years... which is actually not a feature on the government site! Rather, you have to load two reports and make graphs and things yourself. This

Given it's Just A Bunch of CSV files, we can just break this down, and, use chart.js to render said data; and save someone else the headache in future. It's not that complex, and I threw most of this together in an evening.

The only real interesting technical thing to note was how I chose not to solve doing search in the API I threw together, and instead opted for just sending the browser every employer's name and their corresponding ID. This sounds horrific, but, the data is not particularly large, and, doesn't change for at least a year - so it can be cached pretty aggressively, and, making repeated calls against an API redundant.

You can share links but, given it's just static HTML, don't expect there to be a fun embed on social media / Slack / Discord. Otherwise, I hope the tool is helpful to someone :)
